Privacy Policy for Color Sort

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Color Sort is a mobile puzzle game published by IsakovGames ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when you use the game.

1. Game progress

The game stores information such as your current level, puzzle progress, achievements, and preferences locally on your device. This information is used to restore your progress and settings. We do not operate a user account system for this game.

2. Advertising

Color Sort uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including optional rewarded ads. Google and its partners may collect and process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostic data, subject to applicable settings, consent, and law. Please review Google's Privacy Policy for more information.

3. In-app purchases

The game may offer a purchase to remove advertisements using Google Play Billing. Payments are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your full payment card information. Google may process purchase and transaction information according to its own privacy practices.

4. How information is used

Information processed through the game and its service providers may be used to deliver game functionality, display and measure advertisements, process purchases, prevent fraud, and maintain service security and reliability.

5. Sharing and third parties

Google AdMob and Google Play services may process information as independent service providers under their applicable policies. We do not sell game progress stored locally on your device.

6. Data retention and your choices

Locally stored game data generally remains on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the game, subject to Android backup settings. Advertising and purchase-related data processed by Google is subject to Google's retention policies and controls. You can manage advertising preferences through your device and Google settings.

7. Children

Color Sort is a general-audience puzzle game. If the game is offered to children, we will apply the relevant Google Play and advertising requirements, including age-appropriate ad handling and consent where required. Parents and guardians may contact us with privacy questions.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to limit data processing to what is needed for the game and its integrated services. No electronic system can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
